## Computational Mechanics • Structural Engineering • Embodied Carbon Design

A portfolio and research repository documenting the computational design of structural systems, form-finding, seismic resilience, and embodied carbon optimization in concrete shells and hybrid structural systems.

---

### 🔬 Research Focus

- **Low-carbon structural design** — optimizing concrete shells and hybrid systems for minimal embodied carbon
- **Seismic resilience** — form-finding and optimization of self-centring fuse systems and diagrid structures
- **Computational constitutive modelling** — material behaviour including shape-memory alloys and nonlinear mechanics
- **Lifecycle carbon assessment** — from material production to structural performance over service life

### 🛠️ Tools & Methods

<details>
<summary>Computational Tools</summary>

- **Finite Element Analysis:** OpenSeesPy, CalculiX, Gmsh
- **Form-finding:** Force-density method, Python implementations
- **Optimisation:** NSGA-II, CMA-ES, SciML uncertainty quantification
- **Visualisation:** three.js, PyTorch, Matplotlib
- **Life-cycle Assessment:** Custom Python frameworks for A1-A3 carbon accounting
</details>

<details>
<summary>Design Methods</summary>

- Linear buckling analysis and geometric imperfection studies
- Nonlinear time-history analysis under synthetic earthquake records
- Grouped-cross-validation Gaussian process surrogates
- Pareto optimisation with multi-objective trade-offs (drift vs carbon)
- Validation against benchmark problems and hand calculations
</details>

- **287+ automated tests** running on every push
