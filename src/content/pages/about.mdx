Computational structural mechanics, superelastic alloys, thin-shell stability, and low-carbon seismic systems.

Two years of cyclic-durability testing on superelastic NiTi in precision manufacturing, after a BEng in Civil Engineering at Southwest Petroleum University, led me to build verified mechanics solvers for seismic drift and embodied carbon.

- Precision manufacturing (2024-2026): cyclic-durability testing of superelastic NiTi, micro-CNC metrology, failure analysis.
- Southwest Petroleum University (2019-2023): BEng Civil Engineering, Chinese Government Scholarship, RC seismic thesis on a four-storey frame.
- Technical stack: OpenSeesPy, CalculiX, Python/PyTorch, Gmsh, Three.js, LaTeX.
