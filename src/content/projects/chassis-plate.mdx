## Method

`generate_chassis_plate.py` writes the G-code from the outline, the centre slot, the side windows and the two lightening triangles. `simulate_nc.py` plays the file onto a height map.

## Key result

The simulated plate keeps the 160 by 130 mm outline, the 4 by 50 mm centre slot, four side windows and two triangular cut-outs.

## Open limit

Cast acrylic, thickness taken as 4 mm inside the drawing range of 3 to 5 mm. The simulator does not replace a measured part.
