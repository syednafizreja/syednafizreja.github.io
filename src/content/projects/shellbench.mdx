## Method

Coupled OpenSeesPy RC frames (3-8 storeys, 36 frames) and CalculiX shell floors across 5,184 designs (287 automated tests).

## Key result

Median 5.7% whole-building A1-A3 carbon reduction in 32 of 33 comparable frames (14.3% for floors alone).

## Open limit

Seismic frame reinforcement dilutes floor-only savings.
