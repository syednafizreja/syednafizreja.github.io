## Method

1D Auricchio-Sacco return map (1.85e-10 tangent error), PyTorch inverse ID (7.28% max error at 3% noise), OpenSeesPy 80-canopy 0.40 g benchmark, GP/NSGA-II (0/160 false-safe).

## Key result

Cut mean residual drift (0.0659% to 0.0175%), but raised peak drift (1.79% to 3.40%) and carbon (+620.9 kgCO2e).

## Open limit

1D isothermal model. Next step is 3D thermomechanical and cyclic fatigue coupling.
