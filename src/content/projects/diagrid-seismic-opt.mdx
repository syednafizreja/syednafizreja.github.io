## Question

Can a parametric glulam&#8209;timber diagrid canopy fitted with superelastic NiTi self&#8209;centring fuses hold lower residual drift and whole&#8209;life carbon than one with equal&#8209;strength yielding steel fuses. Can a learnable surrogate recover that answer with zero false&#8209;safe designs?

## Findings in brief

A single pipeline runs the whole loop: an exact NiTi constitutive model, parametric geometry, nonlinear OpenSeesPy time&#8209;history analysis, whole&#8209;life carbon, a grouped&#8209;cross&#8209;validation Matérn&#8209;5/2 Gaussian&#8209;process surrogate, NSGA&#8209;II optimisation and a differentiable PyTorch inverse identifier. The surrogate's Pareto front is re&#8209;run exactly in OpenSeesPy, and none of the certified designs is false&#8209;safe. The study surfaces a real trade&#8209;off: an equal&#8209;strength NiTi substitution lowers residual drift, but its 40 GPa modulus against steel's 200 GPa makes the system more flexible, so peak drift rises to 3.40% against steel's 1.79% and lifecycle carbon rises with it. Self&#8209;centring therefore pays only as the output of the constrained, uncertainty&#8209;aware (95% UCB) optimisation, not as a drop&#8209;in part. The workbench exposes the model, the replay and the charts in the browser.

## Approach

- **Constitutive.** A one&#8209;dimensional Auricchio&#8209;Sacco NiTi model with an analytic tangent and Clausius&#8209;Duhem dissipation monitoring.
- **Structure and loading.** The glulam&#8209;timber lattice and steel tree columns are elastic beam&#8209;column elements with P&#8209;Delta geometric transforms; nonlinearity is concentrated in the corotational truss fuses, a self&#8209;centring NiTi element (SelfCentering) against a yielding steel element (Steel02). Loading is synthetic spectrum&#8209;compatible ground motions, static gravity and wind&#8209;uplift equilibrium, run in nonlinear 0.40&#8202;g time&#8209;history.
- **Geometry and carbon.** A parametric diagrid generator with four branching tree columns, and whole&#8209;life carbon as A1&#8211;A3 plus a design&#8209;earthquake repair term, sampled over an 80&#8209;design Latin hypercube.
- **Learning.** A grouped&#8209;cross&#8209;validation Gaussian&#8209;process surrogate returns a mean and uncertainty for every KPI; NSGA&#8209;II draws the Pareto front; a differentiable PyTorch model recovers the NiTi parameters from noisy cyclic data with bootstrap confidence intervals.

## Validation

Every quantitative claim is a pytest gate. The algorithmic tangent matches central differences to under 1.9 × 10&#8315;&#185;&#8304; relative, dissipation stays within the Clausius&#8209;Duhem bound, nonlinear time&#8209;history converges on every record, the grouped&#8209;cross&#8209;validation surrogate's false&#8209;safe rate falls to zero at the 95% upper confidence bound, and the identifier recovers all six NiTi parameters to within 7.28% worst case.

## What this does not show

- The surrogate is trustworthy only inside the sampled design envelope; the 95% UCB is a safety margin, not a guarantee.
- The frame members are elastic beam&#8209;column elements, so member yielding, local buckling and connection damage are not modelled; frame nonlinearity is concentrated in the truss fuses through return&#8209;mapping, not distributed plasticity.
- Ground motions are deterministic synthetic records, not site&#8209;specific hazard.
- Carbon covers A1&#8211;A3 and one repair term; construction, transport and end&#8209;of&#8209;life are excluded.
- The seismic replay amplifies nodal displacements for legibility, so it is not to scale.
