## Question

Can a parametric glulam&#8209;timber diagrid canopy fitted with superelastic NiTi self&#8209;centring fuses hold lower residual drift and whole&#8209;life carbon than one with equal&#8209;strength yielding steel fuses — and can a learnable surrogate recover that answer with zero false&#8209;safe designs?

## Findings in brief

A single pipeline runs the whole loop: an exact NiTi constitutive model, parametric geometry, nonlinear OpenSeesPy time&#8209;history analysis, whole&#8209;life carbon, a Matérn&#8209;5/2 Gaussian&#8209;process surrogate, NSGA&#8209;II optimisation and a differentiable PyTorch inverse identifier. The surrogate's Pareto front is re&#8209;run exactly in OpenSeesPy, and none of the certified designs is false&#8209;safe. The workbench exposes the model, the replay and the charts in the browser.

## Approach

- **Constitutive.** A one&#8209;dimensional Auricchio&#8209;Sacco NiTi model with an analytic tangent and Clausius&#8209;Duhem dissipation monitoring.
- **Multi&#8209;hazard.** Synthetic spectrum&#8209;compatible records, static gravity and wind&#8209;uplift equilibrium, and nonlinear 0.40&#8202;g time&#8209;history integration.
- **Geometry and carbon.** A parametric diagrid generator with four branching tree columns, and whole&#8209;life carbon as A1&#8211;A3 plus a design&#8209;earthquake repair term, sampled over an 80&#8209;design Latin hypercube.
- **Learning.** A Gaussian&#8209;process surrogate returns a mean and uncertainty for every KPI; NSGA&#8209;II draws the Pareto front; a differentiable PyTorch model recovers the NiTi parameters from noisy cyclic data with bootstrap confidence intervals.

## Validation

Every quantitative claim is a pytest gate. The algorithmic tangent matches central differences to under 1.9 × 10&#8315;&#185;&#8304; relative, dissipation stays within the Clausius&#8209;Duhem bound, nonlinear time&#8209;history converges on every record, the surrogate's false&#8209;safe rate falls to zero at the 95% upper confidence bound, and the identifier recovers all six NiTi parameters to within 7.28% worst case.

</More>

## What this does not show

- The surrogate is trustworthy only inside the sampled design envelope; the 95% UCB is a safety margin, not a guarantee.
- Ground motions are deterministic synthetic records, not site&#8209;specific hazard.
- Carbon covers A1&#8211;A3 and one repair term; construction, transport and end&#8209;of&#8209;life are excluded.
- The seismic replay amplifies nodal displacements for legibility, so it is not to scale.
