## Method

Sixteen sliders set the plan, the rise and the skylights of a tapered canopy on tree columns. The page recomputes volume, embodied carbon, deflection and utilisation as the sliders move.

## Key result

The prototype is a way to see the trade. Presets recall a hypar, a dune roof and a wing roof. Nothing on the page is a CalculiX or OpenSees result.

## Open limit

Screening only. A close comparison still needs the shell solvers used on ShellForm and ShellBench.
