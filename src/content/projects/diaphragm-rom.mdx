## Method

CalculiX 2.23 S8R shells set the diaphragm stiffness. OpenSeesPy fiber sections then run the nonlinear time history on seven reinforced-concrete archetypes.

## Key result

588 of 605 runs converged. A rigid diaphragm underpredicts mid-span drift. Out-of-plane w-locking underpredicts it as well. The X-strut model stays on the conservative side. The ductility-adjusted two-degree-of-freedom CQC fit has R^2 0.715.

## Open limit

The record is the 11 site-class B motions at 0.25 g and 0.35 g. It is not a design check for one building.
