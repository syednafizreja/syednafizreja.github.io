## Method

Gravity versus seismic force-density form-finding (168 designs, 0-0.6 g) plus a Three.js solver matching within 10^-13 m.

## Key result

79 of 84 paired carbon comparisons stayed within 5%. Buckling and minimum thickness governed.

## Open limit

Pure compression form-finding loses leverage under lateral inertia.
