## Method

168 feasible designs: 84 gravity-only and seismic-aware pairs from 0 to 0.6 g, with 125 automated tests. Dataset 10.5281/zenodo.24281917. Submitted to Structures.

## Key result

79 of 84 pairs stay within 5% carbon. Linear buckling governs all 84 corner-supported shells. The 50 mm minimum thickness governs all 84 edge-supported shells.

## Open limit

Five crossings are one 5 mm step at lambda_1 = 5.0. That step vanishes on the 61x61 mesh and at lambda >= 3 or lambda = 8.
